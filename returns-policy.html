CUSTOMER CARE

Returns & Exchanges

At BAFFI Classic Apparel, we want you to be satisfied with your purchase. If there is a problem with your order, please contact us so we can review it.

Reporting a Problem

Please contact us as soon as reasonably possible if you receive an incorrect, damaged, defective, or materially different item from the one you ordered. Include your order details and, where helpful, photographs of the item.

Customer-Service Requests

For voluntary returns or exchanges, please contact BAFFI within three days of receiving your order. Tell us your reason for the request and provide your order details.

This three-day customer-service request period does not limit or replace any cancellation, return, refund, or other rights available to you under applicable Ghanaian law.

Review and Resolution

We will review each request and explain the available resolution. Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, this may include an exchange, replacement, repair, or refund.

Refunds

Where a refund is due, we will communicate the process and expected timeframe with you. Any applicable refund and return arrangements will follow the law.

Contact Us

To discuss a return, exchange, or refund, contact BAFFI through our official WhatsApp channel.

CUSTOMER PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

BAFFI Classic Apparel respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you browse our website or place an order with us.

Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with BAFFI, we may collect your name, phone number, delivery location, order details, products purchased, order value, and messages you send to us.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process and confirm orders, arrange delivery, communicate with you, respond to enquiries, handle returns or refunds, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Sharing Your Information

We may share the information necessary to fulfil your order with relevant delivery providers or service providers. We do not sell your personal information.

WhatsApp and External Services

If you contact us or place an order through WhatsApp, your communications are also subject to the applicable terms and privacy practices of WhatsApp and its service provider.

Protecting Your Information

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including applicable recordkeeping and legal requirements.

Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information or ask us to correct inaccurate information. You may also raise concerns about how your information is handled.

Contact Us About Privacy

For privacy questions or requests, contact BAFFI through our official WhatsApp channel.
